#### 🔬 Research & writing

- **[Enterprise AI Observability](https://alexkalyvas.substack.com/p/trends-challenges-and-opportunities)** *(Stanford GSB · Independent Research 390 · Dec 2025)* — Primary research from 100+ AI engineers, founders, and researchers. Headline finding: 60% of AI-native companies already run agents in production — far ahead of McKinsey's enterprise numbers — and the next bottlenecks won't be hallucinations but **privacy/security, observability, and continual learning**. Published as a three-part Substack series.
- **[Enterprise AI Time-to-Value](https://alexkalyvas.substack.com/p/how-long-does-enterprise-ai-take)** *(Stanford GSB · Independent Research 390 · Mar 2026)* — Follow-up paper on what moves enterprise AI from pilot to production. Only **14% of practitioners reach measurable impact in under a month**, yet 50% expect sub-one-month TTV to be standard by 2027 — a 4× expectation gap. Argues that implementation quality, not model quality, is the next moat: forward-deployed engineering, trust transfer, and measurement discipline beat raw capability.
- **[Rosetta Sycophant](https://rosettasyncophant.streamlit.app)** *(Stanford GSB · AI and Power · Winter 2026)* — Live tool with Barry Thrasher and Humzah Khan that detects identity-based bias in AI translation. Same source text, two user profiles → "invasion" vs "intrusion." Built to make sycophancy in cross-language contexts visible and quantifiable; demoed on January 6th reporting from Russian-language sources.
- **Haptica — Tactile Intelligence for Manufacturing** *(Stanford GSB · MKTG 321 · Mar 2026)* — With Devanshi Mehta, Grace Stayner, Paola Peraza Calderon, and Facundo Tosi. End-to-end ML proposal for a wearable tactile-sensor company verifying assembly-line connector seating. Trained per-connector classifiers (Random Forest / Gradient Boosting / SVM) on 16 hand-crafted features from pressure traces; F1 0.67–0.83 across three connector types. Quantified the prize: non-electromechanical assembly failures drive **$14B/year in U.S. recall costs**, and even catching 10% of escapes at a single OEM avoids $50M+ annually.
- **Strike GTM Repositioning** *(Stanford GSB · GTM Project · Dec 2025)* — With Sachin Khurana and Christian Gallo. Strategy recommendations for a Series-A AI pentesting platform (~$3M ARR), grounded in 8 proprietary interviews with the CEO, CFO, and PMs from competitors XBow and Horizon3. Core thesis: shift from per-hour pricing to per-asset PTaaS, double down on LATAM financial services, and position as **hybrid AI + human oversight** against fully-autonomous entrants.
